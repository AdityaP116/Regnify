## Brand & Style

This design system pairs the sobriety of legal publishing with the sharpness of modern data synthesis. Designed for general counsels, chief compliance officers, and risk directors, the interface projects quiet authority, methodical order, and intellectual clarity. Rather than generating alarm, the system distills shifting regulatory landscapes into decisive operational intelligence.

The visual style unites **Editorial Authority** with **Analytical Precision**:
- **Editorial cadence:** Deep serifs for lead headlines convey legislative gravity, sustained institutional knowledge, and statutory context.
- **Analytical architecture:** Dense, hyper-clean sans-serif mechanics handle compliance metadata, statutory cross-references, timeline trackers, and granular impact evaluations.
- **Minimal, structured surfaces:** High-contrast text on serene, tinted white grounds, separated by crisp hairline rules rather than heavy drop shadows.
- **Cognitive containment:** Complex legislative texts, AI syntheses, and operational directives are segmented into distinct visual regimes to ensure non-ambiguous provenance and rapid triage.

## Colors

The palette balances administrative trustworthiness with high-efficiency information parsing. Color is deployed functionally to delineate primary authority from interpretive analysis.

### Structural Palette
- **Primary Teal (`#115E59`):** The institutional core. Used for brand markers, key statutory anchors, dominant callouts, and primary actions.
- **Accent Teal (`#14B8A6`):** The interactive indicator. Used for progress track heads, focus outlines, selected tab lines, and critical data visualizations.
- **AI / Synthesis Indigo (`#6366F1`):** Reserved exclusively for automated synthesis, semantic parsing, and machine-derived risk evaluations. Separates algorithmic interpretation from statutory fact.
- **Primary Ink (`#17201E`):** A dense forest-slate neutral, offering high contrast without the optical fatigue of raw black (`#000000`).
- **Muted Slate (`#52615D`):** For secondary metadata, cross-reference annotations, and table auxiliary strings.
- **Canvas Base (`#F7F9F8`):** A faint, parchment-leaning cool grey that eliminates stark glare.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure white used for content cards, data tables, and modal documents.
- **Border Neutral (`#E2E8E5`):** Subtle structural lines for rule-based demarcation.

### Functional States
- **Success / Verified (`#15803D`):** Formal compliance verification, gazetted status, reconciled legal requirements.
- **Warning / Review Needed (`#B45309`):** Approaching statutory deadlines, pending rule-making notices, cross-jurisdictional divergence.
- **Critical / Exposure (`#B91C1C`):** Non-compliance triggers, expired grace periods, strict civil/criminal liability updates.
- **Metadata Slate (`#64748B`):** Jurisdictional codes, gazette numbers, and administrative timestamps.

## Typography

The type system creates an intentional dialog between legislative prose and analytical data.

- **Newsreader** handles primary headings and long-form narrative intelligence briefs. Its classic proportions reinforce judicial credibility and deliberate reading speed.
- **Manrope** provides a technical, geometric counterweight for body copy, tabular records, audit feeds, and operational instructions. Its geometric curves maintain legibility at micro scales.
- **Tabular Numerics:** Enable `font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1` for all dates, citation sections, article markers, penalty metrics, and confidence percentages to ensure absolute grid alignment down data columns.
- **Metadata Badges & Hierarchy:** Micro-labels utilize uppercase styling with generous tracking (`0.06em`) to anchor complex comparative panels without demanding visual priority over headline prose.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is constructed around an asymmetric fluid grid, referencing scholarly journals and legal case files.

- **Desktop Framework:** 12-column layout with fixed gutters (`1.5rem`) and adaptive canvas margins (minimum `2rem`). Central reading and intelligence panes are restricted to a maximum readable measure of `72ch` to preserve executive scanning efficiency.
- **Comparative Multi-Column Grids:** Statutory diff views split into dedicated 6-column balanced blocks with strict row-height synchronization for side-by-side legal text auditing.
- **Compact Breakpoints:**
  - **Desktop (1280px+):** Full analytical triage (Navigation Sidebar, Primary Dossier, Contextual Intelligence Utility Panel).
  - **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** Contextual panel collapses into an expandable slide-over drawer; main grid compresses to 8 columns.
  - **Mobile (<768px):** Single-column stacked stream. Multi-column diff tables transform into tabbed "Original / Revised" toggles.
- **Rhythm Rules:** All layout heights, margins, and gaps are calculated in multiples of `0.25rem` (4px baseline), ensuring consistent alignment across dense data modules.

## Elevation & Depth

To maintain an uncluttered and authoritative environment, this design system minimizes heavy drop shadows, relying instead on tonal layering, calibrated stroke delineations, and crisp surface tiers.

- **Level 0 (Document Canvas):** `#F7F9F8`. The resting desk surface across which individual intelligence briefs and dossiers are organized.
- **Level 1 (Dossier Surfaces & Analytical Cards):** `#FFFFFF` paired with an ultra-fine border (`1px solid #E2E8E5`). Zero shadow elevation during resting state to preserve a flat, architectural presence.
- **Level 2 (Active Focus & Flyout Panels):** `#FFFFFF` framed by a dual boundary: `1px solid #E2E8E5` and a subtle, high-diffusion ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(17, 94, 89, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(23, 32, 30, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Overlays & Critical Statutory Breaches):** Background dimming via `rgba(23, 32, 30, 0.4)` overlaid with white sheets carrying high-definition edge definition: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(17, 94, 89, 0.12)`.
- **Data Callout Insets:** For AI extracts, legal quoting, and code fragments, an inset tonal wash is applied using `#F1F5F4` with a crisp `3px` solid border on the left margin using the context's thematic color (e.g., `#6366F1` for AI extracts, `#115E59` for statutory law).

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary uses restrained, tight corners (`roundedness: 1`, 0.25rem base radius). This precision reflects legal certitude and avoids overly informal, play-oriented SaaS aesthetics.

- **Interactive Controls (Inputs, Primary Buttons, Chips):** Radius set to `0.25rem` (4px). Clean, crisp corners signal functional rigor.
- **Cards, Panels, and Compare Tables:** Radius set to `0.5rem` (`rounded-lg` / 8px). Subtle softening prevents visual fatigue when managing arrays of simultaneous alerts.
- **Status Pills and Categorical Nodes:** Fully rounded (`rounded-full` / 9999px) strictly for micro-status indicators (e.g., "In Effect", "Pending Review") to differentiate categorical data from actionable input boxes.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#115E59` fill, white `#FFFFFF` text, `0.25rem` radius, `0.8125rem` semi-bold Manrope. Hover: `#0F766E` with subtle scale transition. Active: `#134E4A`.
- **Secondary / Actionable Stroke:** `#FFFFFF` ground, `1px solid #E2E8E5`, `#17201E` text. Hover: `#F7F9F8` background with `#115E59` border.
- **AI Action Variant:** Inset tint `rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.08)`, border `1px solid rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.3)`, text `#6366F1`. Hover: `rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.15)`.

### Chips & Badges
- Strict categorical distinction using four defined archetypes:
  - **Official Source Badge:** Outlined, slate border `#E2E8E5`, text `#64748B`, icon representing original gazette or governing body.
  - **AI Interpretation Badge:** `#EEF2FF` fill, `#6366F1` text, crisp intelligence sparkle glyph.
  - **Business Relevance Badge:** Tinted primary `#CCFBF1`, text `#115E59`, percentage correlation token.
  - **Required Action Badge:** `#FEF3C7` fill, text `#B45309`, deadline icon.

### Form Inputs & Checkboxes
- **Input Fields:** `#FFFFFF` background, `1px solid #E2E8E5`, font `Manrope`, placeholder `#52615D`. On focus: `1px solid #14B8A6`, paired with an outer glow of `0 0 0 3px rgba(20, 184, 166, 0.15)`.
- **Checkboxes:** Standard `16x16px` box with `0.125rem` (2px) radius. Checked: `#115E59` fill with a crisp white tick mark.

### "Why This Matters to You" Intelligence Block
- A high-visibility analytical container. Outer container uses a clean white ground framed by an accented left rail (`3px solid #14B8A6`).
- **Sequential Step Flow:** Displays horizontal connected nodes tracing impact:
  1. *Government Notification* (`#64748B`)
  2. *Business Profile Match* (`#115E59`)
  3. *Specific Impact* (`#17201E`)
  4. *Required Action* (`#B45309`)
  5. *Statutory Deadline* (`#B91C1C`)
- Typography shifts inside the card: The executive takeaway is rendered in `Newsreader` (`headline-sm`), while the underlying statutory proof sits below in `Manrope` (`body-sm`).

### Regulatory Process Tracker Nodes
- A horizontal pipeline visualizing the intelligence chain:
  `DISCOVER` &rarr; `UNDERSTAND` &rarr; `CLASSIFY` &rarr; `MATCH` &rarr; `EXPLAIN` &rarr; `ALERT` &rarr; `ACT` &rarr; `TRACK`
- Unprocessed states: `#E2E8E5` track lines, `#52615D` micro-labels.
- Active node: Pulsing `#14B8A6` circular target with high-contrast text.
- Completed node: Deep `#115E59` circular tick mark.

### Statutory Diff & Comparative Audit Table
- Alternate rows with zero heavy zebra tinting; row separators use clean hairline dividers (`1px solid #E2E8E5`).
- Left column (Pre-amendment): subtle crimson text wash on deleted provisions (`color: #B91C1C; background: #FEF2F2`).
- Right column (Post-amendment): emerald green text wash on newly gazetted text (`color: #15803D; background: #F0FDF4`).
- All citations are pinned to tabular figures with immediate cross-link markers to original regulatory sources.
