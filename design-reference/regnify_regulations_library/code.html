1. Statutory Context & Rationale: The Maharashtra state government has tightened workplace safety enforcement following recurring incidents in pressurized hydraulic circuits and stamping presses. DISH is replacing retroactive, paper-based compliance logs with tamper-proof automated logging. For your Chakan plant operations, this shifts compliance from an administrative filing exercise into an IoT telemetry requirement integrated directly into plant control systems.

2. Practical Engineering Impact: Rather than relying on external inspectors visiting twice a year, your engineering team must install certified calibration data loggers onto all Schedule II heavy press machinery. These sensors must capture cycle counts, pressure thresholds, and maintenance lockouts. Furthermore, each unit's calibration must be recertified with an embedded GPS timestamp verified through the DISH digital portal by an authorized instrument technologist.

3. Legal Liability & Immediate Action: Continuing to rely solely on manual Form 8 logs past the 15 November 2025 threshold will constitute a non-compliance event under Section 92 of the Factories Act, 1948. Penalties include immediate daily compounding fines and potential provisional operational suspensions for unmonitored hydraulic press units. You should initiate hardware quotes immediately to meet the integration lead times.
